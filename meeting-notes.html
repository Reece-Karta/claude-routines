Karta-NX IEC 60601 Certification Pathway with Melina
27 Aug 2026, 7:00 AM GMT+10
Reece Munro Melina (Mecca)

Summary

  • Karta-NX is a non-powered cart; Ultralife's X5 power system already holds its own IEC 60601 certification, but Australian tender processes are asking for the cert on the cart itself.
  • Mecca can evaluate the non-powered cart against IEC 60601-1 with mechanical tests only (stability, sliding, label durability/legibility) — physical testing is identical regardless of documentation depth.
  • Two deliverable options: a 1-2 page letter report (under $1,000) vs. a full ~150-page Test Report Form (~$8,000), of which ~85% would be not applicable for a non-powered cart.
  • No additional 60601-1 testing needed when combining cart and power system, provided mechanical testing covers all mounting configurations and the cart adds no electrical components — this differs from Australia's AS/NZS 3551, which requires combined testing.
  • Labels need updating regardless (a patent number must now be visible); label durability testing is worth including in scope, and failed labels wouldn't require full cart re-submission.
  • NRTL/UL marking is a separate North America-only program requiring ongoing audits — not in scope for now, but worth investigating given Karta's new US entity.

Action Items

  • Melina: Send a quote to Reece covering the letter report as a baseline.
  • Decide internally on letter report vs. full TRF over the next few days before confirming scope with Mecca.
  • Melina: Investigate whether NRTL/UL component certification has precedent for non-powered carts and report back.

Key Decisions

  • Leaning toward the letter report over the full TRF, given how electrical-heavy the standard's full documentation is relative to a non-powered cart.
  • Label durability testing will be included in the scope of the Mecca engagement.
  • NRTL/UL marking confirmed out of scope for now.
